# The Veteran Collective — Persona System

Extracted from an earlier "Stack the Magic" business strategy doc (a
faceless-editorial affiliate content site concept, tangential to this app —
reviewed 2026-09-15). The persona system is the one piece worth carrying
directly into the app.

## How this maps to the app

The onboarding/recommendation flow already has a "Veteran Collective"
concept (see the original product scoping notes). This roster gives it
real content: attribute recommendation-card output to whichever persona
is the relevant specialist, instead of a generic "recommended for you."

Examples:
- A stroller-friendly route → attributed to **The Stroller Scientist**
- A dietary-safe dining pick → verified by **The Allergy Advocate**
- A Lightning Lane sequencing suggestion → from **The Logistics Architect**
- A large multi-generational group's day plan → from **The Grand-Stacker**

A recommendation could carry a small "Verified by [Persona]" tag the way
the original content format did — gives the engine's output a voice
instead of reading like a black box.
