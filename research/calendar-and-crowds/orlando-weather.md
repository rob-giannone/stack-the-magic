# Orlando Weather by Month

## Summary
Orlando has a humid subtropical climate: winters (December-February) are mild and relatively dry with highs in the low-to-mid 70s°F, while summers (June-September) are hot, muggy, and rainy, with highs near 90-92°F and near-daily afternoon thunderstorm potential in peak months. Spring (March-May) and fall (October-November) are transitional and are generally considered the most comfortable, lower-humidity windows for outdoor theme-park days.

**Rain and humidity patterns:**
- **Driest, least humid months:** January and February have the fewest rain days (roughly 6-6.5 per month) and the lowest humidity, with February being the driest/least muggy month of the year.
- **Transitional months (March-May):** Rain days increase gradually (about 7.5-9.5 per month) and humidity starts rising noticeably by May.
- **Peak wet season (June-September):** This is Florida's classic thunderstorm season — expect roughly 15-20 rain days per month, typically in the form of a brief, intense afternoon/early-evening thunderstorm rather than all-day rain. Humidity peaks in July and August, with muggy conditions on most days of the month.
- **Cooling/drying season (October-December):** Rain and humidity drop off through the fall, with November and December among the driest months of the year alongside January/February.

**Practical trip-timing takeaway:** Late fall through winter (November-February) offers the most reliably comfortable, lower-humidity conditions with a lower afternoon-thunderstorm risk, at the cost of shorter days and occasional cold snaps. Summer (June-September) brings the highest heat/humidity index and near-daily afternoon storms, which can affect outdoor queues, fireworks, and parade schedules, but is also when park hours tend to be longest.
